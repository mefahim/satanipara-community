How to use this specimen (QA)

  • Open in Chrome, Safari and an Android phone browser at 360px and 1280px; screenshot every face block.
  • Check the conjunct rows first: ক্ষ ত্র ষ্ট জ্ঞ শ্র ফ্র ব্র হ্র ন্ত ণ্ড স্থ — every conjunct must form as one joined shape, never as a visible hasant (্) plus two separate letters. Check শ্রী (honorific) and the digit ১ in Hind Siliguri.
  • Check the “Font-load status” box at the top: it reports which faces the browser actually loaded. A face that fell back will still read — that is the fallback stack working, not a pass.
  • Compare Anek at 800/condensed (Direction D hero) against Tiro at 400 (broadsheet hero): the difference is weight and width, not size — that is the whole point of Direction D.
  • Rules that never change: never letter-space Bengali, never uppercase Bengali, never fake-bold Tiro (400 only), never fake-italic Anek/Noto.
